Planning Disclaimer

Important information about the home-plan concepts, room layouts, design ideas, illustrations and guidance published on DreamHome Plans.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

DreamHome Plans publishes general educational and inspirational material to help readers explore possible home layouts and interior-design ideas. The content is a starting point for discussion and research, not a substitute for professional advice or project-specific design work.
Important: Do not use a website sketch, illustration, sample dimension or concept plan as a construction drawing, permit submission or instruction to build. Have every proposed design checked and developed by appropriately qualified local professionals before work begins.

1. Not Professional Architectural or Engineering Advice

DreamHome Plans is an informational design resource. Unless a page expressly says otherwise, its articles and examples are not prepared as professional architectural, structural, civil, electrical, plumbing, fire-safety, surveying, energy, accessibility, legal or financial advice. We are not acting as your architect, engineer, building designer, contractor, building inspector or permitting authority.

Reading or using this website does not create a professional-client relationship. Obtain advice from qualified and appropriately licensed professionals who understand your property, project and local requirements.

2. Concept Plans, Drawings and Dimensions

Floor plans, room layouts, furniture arrangements, elevations and 3D visualisations are illustrative concepts. Measurements may be approximate, simplified or based on stated assumptions. They may omit wall build-ups, structural members, insulation, service routes, required clearances, construction tolerances and other details needed for a real project.

Always verify dimensions on site and commission accurate surveys and coordinated construction documents. Check door swings, window positions, circulation, headroom, access, ventilation, daylight and furniture clearances against the actual property and the needs of its occupants.

3. Building Codes, Planning Permission and Local Conditions

Building regulations and planning rules differ between countries, states, provinces, territories, councils and local authorities. Requirements can also change over time. A concept that appears suitable in one location may not be permitted or safe in another.

Before purchasing land, finalising a design or starting work, confirm the applicable zoning and land-use rules, setbacks, easements, site coverage, height limits, permits, energy requirements, fire and life-safety provisions, accessibility standards and inspection requirements with the relevant local authority and qualified professionals.

Site-specific matters such as soil conditions, foundations, drainage, flood exposure, slope, retaining walls, wind, snow, seismic risk, climate, orientation and utility connections require independent assessment. Never assume that a sample plan accounts for these conditions.

4. Materials, Budgets and Cost Information

Any material suggestions, quantities, budgets or cost examples are indicative only unless a page clearly states a verified source, location, date and scope. Actual prices and project costs vary with location, labour rates, taxes, currency, availability, specification, site access, ground conditions, design changes and market conditions.

Obtain current quotations and a project-specific estimate from suitable local professionals and suppliers. Allow for professional fees, approvals, surveys, services, taxes, contingencies and other costs that may not appear in an example.

5. Images and Visualisations

Photographs, renderings and other visual material are provided to communicate style or illustrate an idea. They may not show exact dimensions, construction methods, product specifications, structural details or finishes that can be reproduced in a particular property. Colours and materials may look different on another screen or in different lighting.

Confirm product availability, performance, dimensions, warranties and suitability directly with the manufacturer or supplier before making a purchase or design decision.

We do not control or guarantee the accuracy, availability, safety or policies of third-party websites. Check information directly with the relevant provider or authority.

We aim to make the information useful and clear, but we do not guarantee that every article, dimension, image, link or example is complete, current, error-free or suitable for a particular project. To the extent permitted by applicable law, use of the website and reliance on its content are at your own discretion.
