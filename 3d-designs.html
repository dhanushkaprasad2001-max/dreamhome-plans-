How to review a visual

Five Checks Before You Fall in Love with a Design.

Use this checklist when comparing 3D images, floor plans or early design proposals.

  1. 1. Confirm the overall dimensions.Compare the stated footprint with your land, setbacks and the usable area you actually need.
  2. 2. Check every room connection.Make sure doors, corridors and shared spaces provide sensible routes for daily activities.
  3. 3. Add real furniture sizes.Test beds, wardrobes, sofas, appliances and storage rather than relying on decorative symbols.
  4. 4. Review light, privacy and climate.Window positions, shading, ventilation and roof drainage must suit the actual site.
  5. 5. Request professional drawings.Have a qualified local team coordinate structure, services, safety, approvals and construction details.
Important: 3D pictures are communication tools, not proof that a design is safe, compliant or ready to build. The concepts on DreamHome Plans are for general inspiration only. Building regulations, climate requirements and professional licensing differ across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and local jurisdictions. Obtain site-specific advice and approved drawings from qualified professionals before construction.
