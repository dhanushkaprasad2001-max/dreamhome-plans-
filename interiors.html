Interior Inspiration

Make Each Room Feel Considered, Comfortable and Yours.

Good interiors balance appearance with the way people move, rest, cook, work and store everyday essentials. Use these room-by-room ideas as a practical starting point before choosing furniture, finishes or fittings.

Home / Interior Ideas

Start with How You Use the Space

An attractive room should also work well. Begin by measuring the clear floor area, marking doors and windows, and listing the activities the room needs to support. Then plan the largest furniture pieces, lighting, storage and walking routes before selecting decorative details.

Interior preferences and building conditions vary between homes and countries. Consider the local climate, natural light, ventilation, maintenance needs and the people who will use the space. The suggestions below are flexible design principles, not fixed specifications.

Relax · Gather · Connect

Living Rooms

Arrange seating around the room's main activity, such as conversation, reading or watching television. Keep a clear route between entrances and frequently used areas.

  • Choose a sofa size that leaves usable circulation space.
  • Keep coffee tables within comfortable reach without blocking movement.
  • Combine general lighting with reading or accent lights.

Rest · Store · Recharge

Bedrooms

Plan the bed first, then check access on both sides where possible. Wardrobes, drawers and bedside tables should be usable without obstructing doors or windows.

  • Measure the bed and wardrobe before deciding their positions.
  • Allow space for drawers and wardrobe doors to open.
  • Use layered lighting for reading and getting ready.

Prepare · Clean · Share

Kitchens

Think about the sequence of storing food, preparing ingredients, cooking and cleaning. Keep frequently used items easy to reach and avoid placing obstacles in work routes.

  • Plan practical worktop areas near the main appliances.
  • Check appliance door swings and safe clearances.
  • Choose durable, cleanable surfaces suited to daily use.

Have electrical, gas, plumbing and ventilation arrangements reviewed by qualified local professionals.

Refresh · Organise · Maintain

Bathrooms

Prioritise safe movement, moisture control, easy cleaning and storage for everyday items. Select finishes that are suitable for wet areas and local maintenance conditions.

  • Keep access to the shower, toilet and basin unobstructed.
  • Plan task lighting around mirrors and vanity areas.
  • Provide appropriate ventilation and water-resistant finishes.

Confirm waterproofing, electrical zones and plumbing details with qualified local tradespeople.

Six Practical Interior Planning Checks

  1. Measure before shopping. Record wall lengths, ceiling height, openings and fixed features. Check the route for bringing large furniture into the room.
  2. Plan circulation. Make sure doors, drawers and cupboards can open and that people can move safely around furniture.
  3. Layer the lighting. Combine general room lighting with task lighting for reading, cooking, grooming or working.
  4. Balance storage and open space. Use storage where it solves a real need, while keeping frequently used items accessible.
  5. Choose finishes for their setting. Consider durability, cleaning, moisture, sunlight and the level of maintenance a material requires.
  6. Coordinate services early. Plan sockets, switches, plumbing, heating and ventilation before finalising cabinetry or built-in furniture.
These examples are for general design inspiration. They are not certified architectural, electrical, plumbing or construction documents. Check site measurements, accessibility needs and local building requirements with appropriately qualified professionals before purchasing materials or starting work.
