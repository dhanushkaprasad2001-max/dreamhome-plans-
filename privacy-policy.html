Privacy Policy

This page explains how DreamHome Plans handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes the current features of DreamHome Plans. The website currently provides informational home-design content and does not offer user accounts, on-site payments, comment posting or newsletter subscriptions. We will update this page if those features or our data practices change.

1. Information You Choose to Provide

You can browse the website without creating an account. If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry. We use it to read and respond to your message and to keep a reasonable record of the conversation where needed.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information by email.

2. Technical Information and Hosting

When a website is opened, the hosting provider and other technical services involved in delivering it may automatically process information such as an IP address, browser type, device details, requested pages, approximate timestamps and security logs. This information can be used to deliver the site, maintain reliability, prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical problems. The provider's own privacy terms govern its processing of that information.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that a website or a third-party service may store on your device. They can support basic site functions, remember preferences, measure website use or help deliver advertising. You can usually review or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect features supplied by third parties.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

DreamHome Plans may use third-party services, such as advertising or analytics providers, in the future. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure or personalise advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable choices or consent requirements. We do not control the independent data practices of third-party providers.

For information about Google's advertising technologies and available controls, visit Google's Advertising and Privacy information and Google Ads Settings. These links lead to external websites governed by their own terms.

5. How Information May Be Used or Shared

Information you send directly to us is used to respond to your enquiry and manage the communication. We do not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by hosting, email or other service providers where necessary to operate the website or handle a message. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its visitors or our legal rights.

6. External Websites

Our pages may link to external websites for additional information. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites we do not operate. Please read the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can control many cookie settings through your browser and, where available, through the settings offered by an advertising provider. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us using the address below and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience interested in home design. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter.

9. International Visitors

Visitors may access this website from different countries. Hosting and service providers may process technical information in countries other than the visitor's own, subject to the providers' arrangements and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy when the website, its services or relevant legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page. Please check this page periodically.
